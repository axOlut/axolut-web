Automated business documents

Your quotes, invoices and reports produced in seconds, always clean, always on brand.

Why it matters

Layout work eats time and mistakes creep in. We create polished templates and tools that fill your documents from your data, as PDF or editable Word files. It is the know-how at the heart of FAFIO, which generates print-ready CVs, reports and theses.

Ideal for

  • Practices and service companies
  • Schools and training centres
  • Public bodies and non-profits

What we deliver

  • Quotes and invoices

    Automatic numbering, totals and legal mentions.

  • Reports and minutes

    Cover pages, contents, tables and charts generated for you.

  • Certificates

    Official documents produced in batches, error-free.

  • Document branding

    Consistent Word and PDF templates for the whole company.

A real example: FAFIOOur app that generates CVs, reports and theses as PDF and Word files, on Android and the web.
