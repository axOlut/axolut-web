Frequently asked questions

How much does a website or an app cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the features and the timeline. After a free first call, you get a detailed, itemised quote before committing to anything.

How long does it take to build a website?

Usually two to four weeks for a company website, and a few months for an app or a full platform. The exact schedule is part of the quote.

Do you work with clients outside Cameroon?

Yes. We work remotely with companies in Africa, Europe and North America, in French and in English.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site ships ready for search: fast pages, careful titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap and Google Search Console setup.

What happens after launch?

We stay on hand for fixes, updates and new features, with a support plan that fits your business.
