A digital studio born in Yaoundé, built for the world

axOlut designs digital products to the best international standards, with first-hand knowledge of African markets.

Our story

axOlut started from a simple belief: African businesses deserve digital tools as polished as those of the world’s leading platforms, and suited to their reality: patchy connections, mobile payments, bilingual audiences.

The studio was founded in Yaoundé by Joseph Nguedi Bessala, a solutions architect. It brings design, development and support under one roof, so every project keeps a clear vision from start to finish.

Our own app, FAFIO, proves the point: a product used every day, built with the same care we bring to our clients’ projects.

What guides us

A few simple principles, applied to every line of code and every conversation.

  • Clarity first

    A product should make sense in seconds. If it doesn’t, we simplify it.

  • Solid work

    Clean, tested and documented code that lasts and stays easy to extend.

  • Closeness

    One contact who knows your project, answers quickly and speaks your language.

  • Ambition

    International standards, whether your customer is in Douala, Lyon or Toronto.

Where to find us

We are based in Yaoundé, Cameroon, and work remotely with clients all over the world. We answer Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 7 pm West Africa Time, by WhatsApp, phone or email.
